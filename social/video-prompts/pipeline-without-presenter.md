# Production prompt B — pipeline without presenter

## Master specification

Create a 40-second pure-visual cinematic research sequence. Primary format is vertical 9:16; keep every essential label, graph node, comparison, and final CTA within a centered 16:9 crop-safe region. Use a premium scientific-visualization aesthetic: dark navy spatial field, fine grid, cyan/violet/amber/green accents, red reserved for contradiction, realistic inertia, crisp system-safe typography, stable evidence IDs, and consistent geometry across all scenes. It should feel like a visual research narrative, not a software advertisement or surveillance interface.

## Scene direction

### Scene 1 · 00:00–00:04

Five distinct streams enter from depth: a video-frame ribbon, image tiles, text fragments, an audio waveform, and a sensor trace. Slow forward camera drift as they converge into one axis. On-screen title: “From observations to grounded reasoning.” Align voiceover/subtitle cue 1. Use five soft signal tones and a line-follow transition.

### Scene 2 · 00:04–00:09

The streams crystallize into legible observation cards labeled entity, event, timestamp, confidence, and provenance. Camera makes a measured 20-degree orbital move while the cards lock to a temporal grid. Align cue 2. Maintain the same modality colors and IDs in later scenes. A timestamp expands into the next timeline.

### Scene 3 · 00:09–00:14

Visual frames form a horizontal strip above a cyan temporal reward curve and uncertainty band. A restrained red failure marker pulses once. On-screen question: “What changed over time?” Align cue 3. Glide along the curve; let its final segment transform into an evidence edge.

### Scene 4 · 00:14–00:19

Evidence cards connect chronologically into a temporal graph. Green edges show support, red edges show contradiction, and amber nodes remain unresolved; source IDs, timestamps, and provenance markers stay attached. On-screen question: “What supports or contradicts the hypothesis?” Align cue 4. Push through the hypothesis node.

### Scene 5 · 00:19–00:24

Graph nodes unfold into utility bars with information gain, uncertainty reduction, redundancy, counterfactual contribution, cost, and provenance indicators. One observation rises decisively; repeated frames fade in emphasis but remain visible and traceable. On-screen question: “Which observation actually mattered?” Align cue 5. Morph the highest bar into a selected action card.

### Scene 6 · 00:24–00:30

A bounded agent layer activates around a closed registry. Ranked candidate actions expose value, cost, latency, and reason codes. One registered tool highlights; its structured, cited result updates graph, uncertainty, provenance, and budget. On-screen question: “What should the system inspect next?” Align cue 6. Camera tracks the selected result into the state graph.

### Scene 7 · 00:30–00:35

A red contradiction enters. The current route visibly pauses, reranks, and replans. Positive reward components and penalties rebalance while uncertainty changes; do not imply that positive reward proves truth. On-screen text: “Contradiction → replan → update.” Align cue 7. Split the timeline into two balanced outcome panels.

### Scene 8 · 00:35–00:40

Show two equally credible final states: “Grounded answer with citations” and “Inconclusive stop.” Settle into the exact final CTA: “PersenseAI” / “Multimodal evidence · uncertainty · provenance · bounded agents” / “persenseai.com”. Align cue 8 and hold the final frame for at least 1.2 seconds. No celebratory victory effect.

## Audio and transitions

Use restrained 92 BPM ambient sound: warm low synth, quiet granular motion, and a neutral cadence. Add soft modality tones, card-lock ticks, graph-edge strokes, utility-bar movement, one low contradiction pulse, a bounded-tool confirmation, and a balanced final resolve. Narration remains dominant. All transitions follow a visible data object—stream, timestamp, graph edge, bar, selected card, or split line.

## Negative constraints

No people, fake UI gibberish, random unreadable text, misspellings, invented metrics presented as verified results, analytics-dashboard clichés, code rain, police, military, forensic, surveillance, courtroom, or tactical styling, face scanning, target reticles, weapons, all-knowing imagery, truth-discovery claims, unrestricted autonomous action, product-advertising gloss, exaggerated speed, rapid flashes, camera shake, glitch wipes, broken graph continuity, duplicated IDs, illegible subtitles, or logos other than PersenseAI.
