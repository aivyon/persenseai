# Pipeline carousel slide copy

## 01 — From observations to grounded reasoning

A visual research pipeline for turning video, images, text, audio, and sensor signals into inspectable evidence, uncertainty, and bounded conclusions.

## 02 — Multimodal inputs become observations

Five streams—video, image, text, audio, and sensors—become structured observations with entities, events, time, confidence, and provenance.

## 03 — Visual Reward Timeline

**Question:** What changed over time?

Dense progress and failure signals make temporal behavior visible instead of reducing a sequence to one final score.

## 04 — Evidence Graph Timeline

**Question:** What supports or contradicts the hypothesis?

Observations remain connected to source IDs, frames, timestamps, events, claims, and provenance.

## 05 — Evidence Utility Timeline

**Question:** Which observation actually changed the conclusion?

The system exposes information gain, uncertainty reduction, redundancy, counterfactual contribution, cost, and provenance quality.

## 06 — Evidence Orchestration Agent

**Question:** What should the system inspect next?

A bounded utility-driven agent selects registered tools, updates structured state, tracks budget, handles contradiction, and replans.

## 07 — Answer—or stop honestly

The pipeline returns a grounded answer only when citation, provenance, uncertainty, and contradiction conditions are satisfied. Otherwise, it stops inconclusive.

Deterministic utility-driven policy. No language model is used for planning.
